    A Spanish-first mobile app for U.S. citizenship interview preparation, designed for people who study in Spanish and need confidence answering in English.

    Citizenship preparation is fragmented: official materials, location-dependent answers, and limited help bridging Spanish study with English interview answers.

    User

    Spanish-speaking immigrants preparing civics, the N-400 interview, English reading, and writing in a high-anxiety context.

    Solution

    A bilingual app with civics study, mock interview, N-400 rehearsal, reading/writing practice, audio, and elected-official lookup by ZIP code.

    My role

    Product, design, React Native/Expo, Supabase backend, RevenueCat monetization, launch, and bilingual marketing site.

    Outcome

    Published on iOS and Android with a Pro flow, USCIS-aligned content, and an experience designed for a real bilingual user.

    • Elected officials lookup by ZIP code for location-dependent civics answers

Process

How I work

Principles that guide my product, architecture, and delivery decisions

  • Ship small, learn fast

    I validate with real users before adding debt or unnecessary complexity.

  • Simple, observable, maintainable systems

    I choose architectures teams can operate, debug, and evolve without heroics or tribal knowledge.

  • Modernize without breaking the business

    I migrate legacy platforms pragmatically with feature flags, incremental delivery, and controlled risk.

  • Bilingual product with real context

    I design Spanish and English experiences around users, tone, and cultural constraints—not translation alone.

  • AI and automation with purpose

    I adopt new tools when they remove real friction for users or teams, not because of hype or to replace judgment.
